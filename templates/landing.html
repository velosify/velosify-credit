What we won't tell you

We won't promise a number

Nobody can guarantee a specific score, and it's illegal to try. What we can tell you is exactly what's disputable on your report, and you'll see the result of every challenge we file.

We won't remove accurate items

If an account is genuinely yours, current and correctly reported, it stays. Anyone claiming otherwise is describing fraud, not credit repair.

We won't pretend it's instant

Bureaus get 30 days to investigate. Most clients see the first removals in 30–60 days, and a full case usually runs three to six months.

We won't hide behind an inbox

You can do all of this yourself for free by writing to the bureaus directly. People hire us for the strategy, the follow-through and not having to think about it.
